# Take the Time

A guided greeting card platform for an artist-led card studio that blends intentional, hands-on card creation with an online experience.

## Core idea

People are busy, but meaningful notes still matter. Take the Time helps customers slow down, choose original artist-created card templates, write personal words, and have the final card produced and delivered.

This is not just a standard card store. The in-person studio experience matters: the artist helps people build cards with care. The online product should preserve that feeling with guided choices, warm prompts, thoughtful guardrails, and a simple path from idea to mailed card.

## Working principle

Make it easy for someone to send something that feels personal, intentional, and human.
