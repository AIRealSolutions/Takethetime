# Business Rules — Take the Time

## Core Principle

The platform exists to help people communicate with intention.

Operational decisions should protect:

- emotional authenticity
- artist integrity
- print quality
- customer trust
- thoughtful pacing

## Template Protection

Artist-created templates are intellectual property.

Customers may:

- personalize approved text areas
- upload approved photos
- choose approved options
- request assistance

Customers may not:

- remove artist branding
- destroy layout structure
- export original artwork
- reproduce templates commercially

## Handwritten Integrity

If a product is marketed as:

- handwritten
- hand-addressed
- hand-finished

then fulfillment must honestly reflect that claim.

Transparency is required.

## Order Workflow

Recommended order states:

1. draft
2. submitted
3. awaiting review
4. proof sent
5. awaiting approval
6. approved for print
7. printing
8. shipped or ready for pickup
9. delivered

## Proof Approval

Customers must approve final proofs before printing.

Important approval details:

- spelling
- names
- addresses
- dates
- uploaded photos
- message content

## Emotional Content

The platform may involve:

- sympathy messages
- grief
- apologies
- relationship communication
- milestone life events

Customer content should be treated as sensitive and private.

## AI Usage Rules

AI tools may assist with:

- wording ideas
- grammar improvement
- tone guidance
- reflection prompts

AI tools should not:

- impersonate emotional sincerity
- mass-generate fake intimate messages
- create manipulative emotional content

## Experience Rules

The user experience should avoid:

- aggressive upsells
- fake urgency
- cluttered choices
- distracting notifications
- spam-like promotions

The experience should encourage calm, thoughtful interaction.

## Print Standards

All printed products should maintain:

- safe margins
- bleed support
- readable typography
- image resolution checks
- proof accuracy

## Artist Workload Protection

The artist should have:

- manageable revision limits
- appointment boundaries
- optional premium review pricing
- protected working hours

The business should not create unsustainable emotional labor.
