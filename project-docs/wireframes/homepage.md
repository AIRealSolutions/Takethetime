# Homepage Wireframe — Take the Time

## Goal

Introduce Take the Time as an artist-guided card and handwritten note platform. The page should help visitors understand the emotional purpose, browse cards, or book guided help.

## Navigation

Desktop:

- Home
- Collections
- How It Works
- Guided Sessions
- About the Artist
- Contact
- Start a Card

Mobile:

- Home
- Browse Cards
- Start a Card
- Guided Help
- Contact

Primary CTA:

- Start a Card

Secondary CTA:

- Book a Guided Session

---

## Section 1 — Hero

Headline:

> Take the time to send something that feels personal.

Subheadline:

> Artist-designed cards and guided writing help for thank-you notes, encouragement, celebrations, and meaningful moments.

Buttons:

- Start a Card
- Book a Guided Session

Visual direction:

- Featured card mockup
- Handwritten note texture
- Warm writing desk or studio atmosphere

Design notes:

- Calm, spacious, and warm
- Avoid loud sales language
- Emphasize human care

---

## Section 2 — Why This Exists

Headline:

> We all mean to send the note. This helps you actually do it.

Four support blocks:

1. Life gets busy
2. Words can be hard to start
3. A handwritten note still matters
4. The guided process makes it easier

CTA:

- Find the right card

## Section 3 — Featured Collections

Headline:

Collection cards:

- Thank You

- Just Because

Each collection card includes:

- artwork preview

- browse button

## Section 4 — How It Works

Headline:

> A slower, more thoughtful way to send a card.

Steps:

1. Choose an artist-designed card
2. Write your message with guided prompts
3. Request artist review if needed
4. Approve your proof
5. We produce, prepare, and deliver

CTA:

- Start Your First Card
