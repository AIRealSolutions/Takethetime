# Collections Page Wireframe — Take the Time

## Goal

Help customers browse by intention, occasion, or feeling instead of only browsing products.

The page should feel curated and calm.

---

## Header

Headline:

> Browse by the feeling you want to send.

Subheadline:

> Select a collection, then personalize an artist-designed card with your own words.

Primary CTA:

- Start with a Guided Prompt

Secondary CTA:

- View All Cards

---

## Filters

Recommended filters:

- Occasion
- Tone
- Recipient
- Card format
- Artist favorites
- Photo supported
- Hand-finished available

UX note:

Filters should be helpful but not overwhelming. Keep mobile filters collapsed behind a simple button.

---

## Collection Grid

Each collection card should include:

- artwork preview
- collection name
- emotional description
- number of designs
- browse button

Suggested collections:

## Thank You

Description:

> For gratitude that deserves more than a text.

## Birthday

Description:

> Personal designs for another year, another memory, and another reason to celebrate.

## Encouragement

Description:

> Send strength, hope, and support when someone needs to feel seen.

## Wedding

Description:

> Thoughtful notes for guests, family, and once-in-a-lifetime moments.

## Holiday

Description:

> Seasonal cards that feel warmer than a mass mailing.

## Business Appreciation

Description:

> Personal notes for clients, partners, employees, and community relationships.

## Just Because

Description:

> For the note you have been meaning to send.

---

Copy:

- Start with Guided Help
