# Design Direction — Take the Time

## Brand tone

- Thoughtful
- Warm
- Handcrafted
- Calm
- Elegant
- Authentic

## Visual direction

The design should feel like:
- A modern paper studio
- A creative writing desk
- A boutique stationery shop
- A calm coastal artisan brand

## Color direction

Suggested palette:
- Warm ivory
- Soft white
- Muted navy
- Sand/beige tones
- Dusty sage accents
- Charcoal typography

Avoid:
- Neon colors
- Harsh gradients
- Overly corporate design
- Loud ecommerce banners

## Typography

Primary:
- Elegant serif or soft editorial font

Secondary:
- Clean readable sans-serif

Accent:
- Optional handwriting font for previews only

## Homepage structure

### Hero section
- Emotional storytelling
- Focus on intentional communication
- Showcase original artist work

### Featured collections
- Thank you
- Sympathy
- Birthday
- Encouragement
- Wedding
- Holiday

### Guided experience section
Explain how customers personalize cards.

### Studio story
Highlight the artist and in-person creative process.

### Testimonials
Focus on emotional impact.

## UX guardrails

- Keep checkout simple
- Reduce decision fatigue
- Encourage personalization
- Mobile-first responsive design
- Accessibility-first typography and spacing

## Future design opportunities

- Digital wax seal previews
- Envelope liner customization
- Handwritten font generation
- Voice-to-message drafting
- Memory/journal-inspired workflows
