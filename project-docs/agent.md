# Agent Context — Take the Time

## Mission

Build a guided greeting card and handwritten correspondence platform that helps people slow down and send meaningful notes without losing the emotional connection of traditional correspondence.

The business exists because people are busy, distracted, and often intend to send thank-you cards, encouragement notes, sympathy cards, or personal letters but never make the time.

Take the Time removes friction while preserving sincerity.

## Core Philosophy

The customer is not simply ordering a product.

The customer is creating a moment of connection.

The platform should encourage:

- intentionality
- gratitude
- thoughtfulness
- human connection
- personal expression
- care for others

Technology should assist emotional expression, not replace it.

## What Makes This Different

- Original artist-created card templates
- Guided creative experience
- In-store artist support
- Online personalization
- Calm writing prompts
- Optional artist review
- Physical production and delivery
- Focus on emotional value over mass production

## What This Is

This is:

- an artist-led creative studio
- a boutique card and letter platform
- a guided personal correspondence workflow
- a hybrid online and in-store business
- a relationship-centered product

## What This Is Not

This is not:

- a discount greeting card store
- a generic print-on-demand marketplace
- a meme-card site
- a spammy ecommerce funnel
- a fully automated AI emotional content generator

## User Types

### Customers

- Busy professionals
- Families
- People sending thank-you cards
- Sympathy and wellness message senders
- Holiday and milestone senders
- Business owners thanking clients
- Customers who value meaningful communication

### Artist/Admin

- Uploads and organizes templates
- Helps customers personalize designs
- Manages proofing and fulfillment
- Curates seasonal collections
- Reviews sensitive or premium orders
- Builds workshop and studio experiences

## Product Direction

The experience should feel:

- warm
- calm
- encouraging
- personal
- simple
- creative but not overwhelming
- trustworthy

Avoid:

- loud ecommerce energy
- aggressive upsells
- countdown timers
- cluttered interfaces
- forced urgency
- over-automation that removes authenticity

## Suggested Stack

- Frontend: Next.js
- Styling: Tailwind CSS
- Hosting: Vercel
- Database/Auth: Supabase
- Storage: Supabase Storage
- Payments: Stripe
- Printing/Fulfillment: future integration

## AI Usage Guardrails

AI may help users:

- organize thoughts
- overcome writer's block
- suggest wording ideas
- improve clarity
- choose a tone
- reflect on what they want to say

AI should not:

- fake emotional authenticity
- mass-generate generic emotional messages
- remove the sender's personal voice
- create manipulative or insincere content

The final message should always feel like it came from the sender.

## Operational Guardrails

- Protect artist-created intellectual property.
- Lock core artwork from destructive editing.
- Preserve print quality and safe margins.
- Treat personal messages as private content.
- Be truthful about handwritten, hand-finished, or printed fulfillment.
- Maintain clear proof approval workflows.
- Avoid overwhelming customers with too many choices.

## Long-Term Vision

A platform that restores thoughtful communication in a distracted world by blending original art, guided writing, personal care, and physical keepsakes.
