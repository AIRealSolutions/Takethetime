# Agent Context — Take the Time

## Mission

Build a guided greeting card platform that helps people send meaningful handwritten-style cards without losing the emotional connection of traditional correspondence.

## Core philosophy

The platform should encourage intention, thoughtfulness, and slowing down.

The customer is not simply ordering a product.
The customer is creating a moment of connection.

## What makes this different

- Original artist-created card templates
- Guided creative experience
- Human-centered tone
- Blend of physical studio experience and online ordering
- Focus on emotional value over mass production
- Potential handwritten fulfillment and personalization workflows

## User types

### Customers
- Busy professionals
- Families
- People sending thank-you cards
- Sympathy/wellness messages
- Holiday and milestone senders
- Customers who value meaningful communication

### Artist/Admin
- Upload and organize templates
- Help customers personalize designs
- Manage fulfillment workflow
- Curate featured collections
- Build seasonal campaigns

## Product direction

The experience should feel:
- Warm
- Calm
- Encouraging
- Personal
- Simple
- Creative but not overwhelming

Avoid:
- Loud ecommerce energy
- Aggressive upsells
- Cluttered interfaces
- Over-automation that removes authenticity

## Suggested stack

- Frontend: Next.js
- Hosting: Vercel
- Database/Auth: Supabase
- Storage: Supabase Storage
- Payments: Stripe
- Printing/Fulfillment: future integration

## AI usage guardrails

AI should assist creativity, not replace sincerity.

Examples:
- Help rewrite a message gently
- Suggest wording ideas
- Help organize layouts
- Recommend occasion wording

Avoid:
- Fully generic emotional spam
- Fake intimacy
- Manipulative copy

## Long-term vision

A platform that restores thoughtful communication in a distracted world.
