# Objectives — Take the Time

## Primary goal

Create a platform where customers can personalize artist-created greeting cards online while maintaining the emotional authenticity of handwritten communication.

## MVP Objectives

### Phase 1 — Foundation
- Brand identity
- Landing page
- Artist story
- Card collection browsing
- Card detail pages
- Personal message editor
- Cart and checkout
- Order submission workflow

### Phase 2 — Guided personalization
- Occasion prompts
- Writing assistance
- Suggested phrases
- Template personalization
- Handwriting-style font previews
- Scheduled delivery

### Phase 3 — Studio integration
- In-store assisted sessions
- Shared editing experience
- Saved drafts
- Customer accounts
- Premium artist review service

### Phase 4 — Fulfillment automation
- Printing workflows
- Shipping workflows
- Mailing integrations
- Inventory tracking
- CRM and marketing automation

## Business objectives

- Build recurring seasonal customers
- Encourage intentional communication habits
- Create premium perceived value
- Support local/artisan creativity
- Blend digital convenience with physical authenticity

## Success metrics

- Repeat customers
- Cards sent per user annually
- Average personalization length
- Customer retention
- Seasonal campaign conversion
- Customer testimonials and emotional engagement

## Product guardrails

Do not become:
- Generic print-on-demand spam
- Meme-card marketplace
- Low-effort AI card generator
- Coupon-heavy ecommerce experience

The product should feel curated and meaningful.
