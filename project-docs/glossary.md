# Glossary — Take the Time

## Guided Session

A live or in-person collaborative card-building experience where the artist helps the customer create a meaningful card or letter.

## Artist Review

Optional premium review service where the artist reviews wording, layout, tone, and final presentation before printing.

## Concierge Design

A high-touch service where the customer provides a story, occasion, or emotional direction and the artist creates most of the final card experience.

## Reflection Prompt

A guided question shown during personalization to help the customer think more deeply about what they want to say.

Examples:
- What are you grateful for?
- What moment do you want them to remember?
- What do you wish people said more often?

## Template

An original artist-created card design that contains editable personalization zones.

## Editable Zone

An approved area where customers may personalize text, photos, or selected options.

## Locked Artwork

Protected artist-created design elements that customers may not alter.

## Proof

A preview of the customized card approved before printing or fulfillment.

## Hand-Finished

A product partially completed by hand such as handwritten addressing, signatures, embellishments, or artistic finishing touches.

## Studio Experience

The physical in-store creative process where customers receive guided assistance while building cards.

## Emotional Authenticity

The principle that the final message should feel sincere, personal, and human rather than mass-generated or automated.
